# Ex.No.-1.2-Design and Draft the given 2D Sketches in modelling software.

## AIM
 To sketch the given drawing with dimensions using fusion 360 tool.
 
## REQUIREMENTS
 1. Autodesk fusion 360.
 2. Windows 10.
 3. 16 GB of RAM (integrated graphics recommend 1 GB or more).
 4. 2.5 Mbps or faster download; 500 Kbps or faster upload.
 
 ## PROCEDURE
1.Open Autodesk Fusion 360 and create a new design. Select the XY Plane and click on Create Sketch to enter the 2D sketching   workspace.

<img width="569" height="394" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-11 203843" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ad6dab4c-b1ab-4e4e-83fe-39f60d8ec14d" />

2.Draw horizontal and vertical Construction Lines passing through the origin. These lines act as reference axes for drawing    the component accurately and maintaining symmetry.
	
3.Apply the required inner circular radius of R15 to all six outer circular portions. Maintain the outer radii of R21 mm for   the upper and lower circular portions and R20 mm for the left and right circular portions.

4.Use the Line Tool (L) to draw the connecting straight lines between the outer circular portions. These lines form the side   boundaries of the component.

<img width="230" height="292" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-12 215438" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a5d89e74-e3c8-41c6-9dfd-053d91cd69ab" />

5.Select the Arc Tool to draw the curved connecting portions between the circular boundaries. Apply the required R20 mm        radius to the curved portions near the central region.

6.Draw the curved portions around the central circular arrangement using the specified radii of R16, R21 and R32mm. Maintain   the required shape and spacing of the central circular design.

7.Create the two parallel vertical lines near the central lower portion and maintain the specified 6 mm gap between them, as   shown in the reference drawing.

8.Use the MIRROR tool from the Sketch Modify menu to create symmetrical portions of the component. Select the required         sketch geometry and use the vertical construction line as the mirror line.

<img width="283" height="217" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-08 212119" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/dc7d6949-c8fc-4cdb-a269-b880a92b2f89" />


9.Repeat the mirroring operation wherever necessary to complete the symmetrical upper and lower portions of the design.        Check that both sides are properly aligned.

10.Select the TRIM tool from the Modify menu to remove unwanted portions of the overlapping circles and arcs. Carefully         select the extra curves that are not required in the final component.


<img width="140" height="234" alt="Screenshot 2026-10-08 212657" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bdf74302-fcee-4452-bad4-95be6707947f" />


11.Continue trimming the unwanted internal lines and overlapping portions to obtain the required outer boundary and the six     circular openings.

12.Complete the central portion by retaining the required circular boundaries and removing the unwanted portions to form the    six equally arranged openings around the centre.

13.Check the complete sketch and ensure that all six outer circular portions, central openings and connecting boundaries are    properly formed. Verify the radii and dimensions with the given reference drawing.

14.Apply the necessary geometric constraints, such as Tangent, Coincident and Symmetry, wherever required to maintain the       correct geometry.


<img width="277" height="420" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9623f73e-4828-48d9-8f06-357c8e371342" />


15.After completing the 2D drawing, click on FINISH SKETCH to exit the sketching environment. The completed 2D profile is       now ready for three-dimensional modelling.


<img width="227" height="293" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-12 220603" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7cb2deaa-3465-4d17-a675-617090065ae9" />



16.Select the required closed profile of the component and choose the EXTRUDE tool from the Create menu to convert the 2D       sketch into a 3D model.


<img width="209" height="287" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-11 213604" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ecd88e7d-ee27-4b44-a88d-e4eca5adf4f2" />


17.Specify the extrusion distance according to the experiment's modelling instructions. Select the appropriate operation and    click OK to complete the extrusion.

18.Show the finished 3D model with smooth fillets applied to the edges, ready for final review.
